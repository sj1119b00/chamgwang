Prometheus regional enemies and rulers

The human surfaces, skin weights, garment fits, hairstyles, skin maps and
fabric maps are adaptations of the MakeHuman community's HM08 base mesh and
MakeHuman system assets. These art assets are available under CC0 1.0.

Regional weapons, wearable equipment, character casting, color treatments,
skeletal performance, sprite baking and integration were authored for this game.
The rotating codex uses Three.js r174 and GLTFLoader. Its MIT license is in
frame/vendor/three-LICENSE.txt.

Garment movement incorporates five pinned cloth solves in Blender 4.2 LTS.
Every regional human is imported into Blender's mesh/shape-key authoring
pipeline to sculpt the fitted sleeves, knees and facial relief. Regional
fabric studies and their editable Blender scene are in _gen/character-studio.
Close-up face normals, articulated combat equipment and regional court regalia
were authored for Prometheus. An original eight-district embroidery sheet
created with OpenAI image generation is mapped to the fitted costumes, with
matching thread normals and roughness. Its source and prompt are kept in
_gen/character-studio/regional-embroidery.png and regional-embroidery.txt.

Each regional outfit has a dense Blender source with twice-subdivided face
patches, once-subdivided body and garments, and sculpted skin and cloth
relief. Its tangent-space projection is combined with the woven and skin
microdetail. Ambient visibility is sampled against the dressed sculpt with
12 deterministic directions and packed with roughness and metallic response.
The models also carry their regional lamp or transponder emission map.
The original high surfaces and all six PBR channels are reproducible with
_gen/build_campaign_foes.py, cast_geometry_bake.py and cast_baked_maps.py.

The mandibular hinge, lip influences, individual upper and lower teeth,
tongue and finger-joint influences are fitted from the same HM08 rig and
CC0 system assets. The performed jaw, upper lip, nostril and brow shapes,
individual hand spread and grip compression were authored for the regional
combat actions. The original buccal wall and palate link the lips to the
throat. A hair-only tangent-direction chart shares an atlas with the
electrical hardware's separate emissive UV island.

The regional enemies and rulers use retargeted idle and walk performances
from Adobe Mixamo's X Bot, as published in the three.js r174 animation example.
The character rigs, adapted joint curves, cloth response and work/combat
gestures are fitted for Prometheus. The source and its verified digest are
recorded in _gen/mixamo_locomotion.py.
